# Session 5 prompts — the assistant reviews, it does not write

Run all three. Commit version one **before** you run any of them.

**One screen for the group.** Whoever has access is the driver: they type, the group dictates. If
nobody in your group has access yet, share a neighbouring group's driver or ask the facilitator.
Record the driver in the commit message, and swap the seat as access spreads.

## 1 · The gap hunt
> You are reviewing a solution definition written by a junior engineering team. Here is the case
> background, and here is our definition. List every place where we have assumed something the case
> does not support. For each one, quote our line and say what evidence would settle it.
> Do not rewrite our document.

## 2 · The criteria test
> For each acceptance criterion below, say whether it could fail. If it could not fail as written,
> explain why, and give one example of an implementation that would satisfy it while being useless.

## 3 · The missing piece
> Given this problem statement and these stakeholder concerns, name three components or stories
> missing from our definition. For each, say which stakeholder would notice it was missing, and when.

## Before you accept anything it says
- Is it in the case, or did it arrive from a generic best-practice list?
- Can it name the stakeholder who would care?
- Is it a gap, or a garnish that is true of every system ever built?
- Would you have found it yourself in ten more minutes?

**Reject at least one finding and be able to say why.** A group that accepted everything was
transcribing, not reviewing.
