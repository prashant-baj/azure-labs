# Working on this case

## What this repository is
A training engagement for a single client case. `docs/` holds what we learned by asking people.
`specs/` holds what we have decided to build. Neither is fiction — treat `docs/` as evidence.

## House rules for the assistant
- Do not invent facts about the client. If the case does not say it, say that the case does not say it.
- Quote our own lines back to us when you criticise them.
- Prefer "I cannot tell from the case" over a plausible guess.
- Do not add generic best practice unless you can name the stakeholder who would care about it.
- Use the client's words. The glossary below is not a suggestion.

## Conventions
- Requirements are written in EARS form: *When \<trigger\>, the system shall \<response\>*
- Every acceptance criterion must be able to fail
- Every threshold is a decision with a named owner, not a fact
