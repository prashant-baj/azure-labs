# Session 8 prompt — it argues against you

## The attack

> Here is our NFR register and our constraint list, including the platform policy we must work
> inside. **Argue against us.** For each requirement: say whether it is verifiable, whether the
> threshold has an owner, and whether our constraints make it impossible. Name the ones we should
> delete.
> **Do not add new requirements.**

## Then, once

> Which single requirement, if we got it wrong, would hurt us most in the first month after go-live?
> Answer from our register only.

## Before you accept any of it
- **Deleting is a win.** A register of four lines you can verify beats twenty you cannot.
- Did it obey the restriction, or has it quietly added requirements? Strike those out.
- **Defend at least one** of its objections. If you deleted everything it questioned, you were not deciding.

If you leave with more lines than you started with, something has gone wrong.
