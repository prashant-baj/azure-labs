# Review findings — <slice>

*`specs/review/findings.md`. Your scribe types each finding here as the stakeholder roles read them out on the
board, then the group decides them in triage with `/triage-review`, and fixes them in the next session. A finding
names what was seen, the evidence, the risk, and what to do — never who did it.*

**Severity:** `high` — would block go-live, or would harm the client · `medium` — fix before the next release ·
`low` — an improvement.

**Decision:** `fix now` (first thing in the next session: a test named after the finding, then the fix) · `spec change` (the spec, the
strategy or an ADR changes first) · `backlog` (Sessions 19–20, with an owner) · `accepted` (with who agreed).
