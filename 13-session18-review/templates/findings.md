# Review findings — <slice>

*`specs/review/findings.md`. Written down from the review board's scorecard in step 4, then decided in step 5 with
`/triage-review`. A finding names what was seen, the evidence, the risk, and what to do — never who did it.*

**Severity:** `high` — would block go-live, or would harm the client · `medium` — fix before the next release ·
`low` — an improvement.

**Decision:** `fix now` (today: a test named after the finding, then the fix) · `spec change` (the spec, the
strategy or an ADR changes first) · `backlog` (Sessions 19–20, with an owner) · `accepted` (with who agreed).
