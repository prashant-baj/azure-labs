with `/triage-review`. Feedback is evidence about the spec: some of it is a defect, some of it is a requirement
nobody wrote down, and some of it is a new request that needs a decision, not a promise.*

**Type:** `defect` (the slice does not do what the spec says) · `spec change` (the spec was wrong or silent) ·
`new request` (outside this slice) · `question` (needs an answer, not code).

**Decision:** `fix now` · `spec change` · `backlog` · `declined` (with the reason, told to the person who asked).
