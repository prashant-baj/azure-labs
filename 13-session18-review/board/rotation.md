# Board rotation — Session 18

Every group presents once and sits on two boards. Seven minutes per slot, in this order:

| Slot | Reviewed | Board | Customer feedback from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Group 1 · Nova Retail | Group 2, Group 3 | the facilitator, as Nova's people |
| 2 | Group 2 · Sterling National Bank | Group 3, Group 4 | the facilitator, as Sterling's people |
| 3 | Group 3 · PureFoods Global | Group 4, Group 5 | the facilitator, as PureFoods' people |
| 4 | Group 4 · Apex Industrial | Group 5, Group 1 | the facilitator, as Apex's people |
| 5 | Group 5 · EcoNova Enterprises | Group 1, Group 2 | the facilitator, as EcoNova's people |

**Each slot:** 3 min the reviewed group shows · 2 min board questions · 2 min customer feedback · then the board
writes its scorecard (3 min, while the next slot starts — one board member writes, the other listens).

**Before your slot as a board:** read the reviewed group's case notes in one minute (lab 12, `case-notes/` — the
slice is in the first lines) and their `specs/design/readiness.md`, which they share on screen or as a link.

**While another board reviews you:** one person presents, one drives the screen, everyone else writes down every
question the board asks. A question you could not answer is a finding, even if the board does not write it.
