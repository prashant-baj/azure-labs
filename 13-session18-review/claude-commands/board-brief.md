Read `specs/design/readiness.md`, the last `confidence.md` (ask the group to paste it if it is not in the repo),
`specs/design/observability.md` and `specs/review/findings.md`.

Write a brief the group can say in **three minutes**, in this order:

1. **One sentence:** what the slice does for the client, in the client's words.
2. **Show it running:** the one request to make live, and what the board should see.
3. **The business number and the technical number**, read off the dashboard — which tile, what it means.
4. **The last deploy scorecard:** CONFIDENT or not, and the row the board should look at.
5. **Our top three gaps**, from the checklist — said before the board finds them. A group that names its own gaps
   earns trust; a group that hides them loses it.

Then list the five questions the board is most likely to ask, with where the evidence is for each (a path, a test,
a job). Do not write slides. Do not soften a gap.
